Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our data architecture and dual login.

Where is the backend and how does data persistence work? ▼

The backend is located in the backend/ directory, powered by Python 3.12, FastAPI, and an ACID-compliant SQLite engine (data/spendsense.db). When deployed on static hosts like Vercel, our browser-native client engine stores data locally in persistent storage with zero loss of features!

How do Customer and Owner logins differ? ▼

On our login screen, simply toggle between Customer Portal and Owner Console. Customers are directed to personal financial dashboards, budget drift monitors, and statement uploads. Owners are granted administrative access to the exact database management console, SQL query runner, and customer registry.

Can I export and back up my entire database? ▼

Yes! In the Owner & Database Studio, you can download a complete JSON or SQLite dump with 1-click under the Operations tab, ensuring you maintain 100% data sovereignty at all times.
